# Image Specification — The World in One Day (27 November 2026)

## Canvas & Aesthetic
* **Dimensions:** 1024 x 1024 pixels.
* **Format:** PNG (or JPEG), strictly under 1 MiB.
* **Style:** Classic newspaper front page on light paper.
* **Color Palette:**
  * Background: Light cream paper (`#F7F2E7`)
  * Text / Ink: Dark ink (`#111111`)
  * Accents / Rules: Deep red (`#A6192E`) for rules, masthead underline, and kickers.
  * Muted Tones: Soft greys and blues for illustrations and world map.
* **Layout Structure:** One clean composition, no photo collage, no garbled text. All text legible at ~300px wide. Serif bold for masthead and headlines; clean sans-serif for kickers, sub-headlines, and list items. Headlines in ALL CAPS, black. Sub-headlines in sentence case, dark grey (`#333333`).
