## Main Stories (9)

### 1. Kuwait Drone Attack Kills Two Italians
* **Event:** A drone attack targeted a Kuwaiti military air-defence site.
* **Place:** Kuwait.
* **Key Facts & Figures:** Two Italian technicians—employed by Rheinmetall to maintain detection equipment—were killed. A Kuwaiti national and a Pakistani national were injured. Italy stated the drone was Iranian-made; Iran denied responsibility and characterized the incident as suspicious.
* **Sources:** 10things.news 27 Nov briefing (sources: Washington Post, CNN); dailysummaryof.com/world/2026-11-27 (first reported 27 Nov 00:00 UTC); clisa.ai/en/brief/2026-11-27.

### 2. Zelenskyy Warns of New Russian Mass Strike; Trump Urges Putin to Pull Back
* **Event:** Warnings of imminent mass airstrikes and international diplomatic appeals.
* **Place:** Ukraine / Europe.
* **Key Facts & Figures:** Ukrainian officials reported that Russia is preparing a new mass strike in the coming days. President Volodymyr Zelenskyy repeated the warning following overnight ballistic missile strikes on Kyiv that wounded seven people. Meanwhile, US President Donald Trump stated he pressed Russian President Vladimir Putin to pull back military aggression across Europe.
* **Sources:** dailysummaryof.com/world/2026-11-27 (first reported 27 Nov 02:14 UTC); 10things.news 27 Nov briefing (sources: Kyiv Independent, Reuters).

### 3. EU Summit Ends Early; €45 Billion Ukraine Loan Delayed
* **Event:** Special European Union summit in Brussels truncated without agreement on financial assistance.
* **Place:** Brussels, Belgium.
* **Key Facts & Figures:** The special EU summit opened on 26 November, cancelled its planned second day, and ended early on 27 November without deciding on a 45 billion euro loan for Ukraine. The decision was pushed to December. Germany insisted that all 27 member states back the loan, while Slovakia, Hungary, and Czechia opposed it.
* **Sources:** clisa.ai/en/brief/2026-11-27; 10things.news 27 Nov briefing (sources: The Guardian, Politico Europe); dailysummaryof.com/world/2026-11-27.

### 4. Thanksgiving Storms Cut Power Across the US
* **Event:** Severe weather disruption across the United States during Thanksgiving.
* **Place:** United States (New York, New Jersey, and other regions).
* **Key Facts & Figures:** Strong winds and heavy storms left nearly a million US homes and businesses without power and caused the rare postponement of the Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade in New York City. Over 2,000 flights were delayed and more than 400 cancelled. New Jersey and parts of New York declared states of emergency. (*Note on figures: one source reported "over 800,000" customers and another "nearly a million"; per verification rules, the front-page image uses no numerical count for power outages.*)
* **Sources:** dailysummaryof.com/world/2026-11-27 (first reported 27 Nov 04:34 UTC); oneturn.news/topic/disaster (27 Nov).

### 5. New Zealand Coalition Falls One Seat Short
* **Event:** Final election results alter parliamentary balance.
* **Place:** Wellington, New Zealand.
* **Key Facts & Figures:** Final results showed the National-led coalition secured 65 of 130 seats—leaving it one seat short of a majority after special votes cost it two seats. Prime Minister Christopher Luxon must negotiate with the centrist Opportunity party to stay in power. The Labour Party won the popular vote but lacked the necessary seats to form a government.
* **Sources:** dailysummaryof.com/world/2026-11-27 (first reported 27 Nov 01:41 UTC).

### 6. Britain Contacts 100,000 Veterans Over Military Readiness
* **Event:** National defense outreach to former military personnel.
* **Place:** London, United Kingdom.
* **Key Facts & Figures:** Britain contacted approximately 100,000 former military personnel to assess their availability for potential recall—the first such initiative since the Cold War. Participation is entirely voluntary, and no immediate mobilisation is planned. Defence Secretary Wes Streeting cited a deteriorating European security environment.
* **Sources:** 10things.news 27 Nov briefing (sources: United24, BBC).

### 7. Congo Ebola Cases Pass 12,000
* **Event:** Escalation of public health crisis and humanitarian disruption.
* **Place:** Democratic Republic of Congo (Ituri province and 7 other provinces).
* **Key Facts & Figures:** Government data confirmed the Ebola outbreak reached 12,021 confirmed cases and 5,904 deaths. Originating in May in Ituri province, the outbreak has spread to 8 of the country's 26 provinces. Renewed fighting severely disrupted containment efforts, forcing response teams to evacuate from several areas.
* **Sources:** 10things.news 27 Nov briefing (sources: AP, CBC).

### 8. US Tariff Plan Rejected by Trading Partners
* **Event:** International pushback against sweeping new US trade duties.
* **Place:** Washington, DC / Global economies.
* **Key Facts & Figures:** China, Japan, and other US trading partners rejected Washington's justification for new tariffs targeting 14 economies that together account for roughly half of global economic output. The duties follow a Section 301 investigation aimed at restoring tariffs previously struck down by the US Supreme Court in February. China faces a 20 percent tariff that could potentially double to 40 percent.
* **Sources:** 10things.news 27 Nov briefing (sources: AP, FT).

### 9. AFP Takes Platform Offline After Cyberattack
* **Event:** Major news agency cyber security incident.
* **Place:** Paris, France / Global.
* **Key Facts & Figures:** Agence France-Presse took its AFP Forum content platform offline following a cyberattack that began at 00:30 GMT on 27 November. The agency is actively investigating the origin and perpetrators of the intrusion. France's cybersecurity chief stated the nation is facing an unprecedented level of threats.
* **Sources:** dailysummaryof.com/world/2026-11-27 (first reported 27 Nov 01:07 UTC).
